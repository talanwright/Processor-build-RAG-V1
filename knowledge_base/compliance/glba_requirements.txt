GRAMM-LEACH-BLILEY ACT (GLBA) COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS

PRIVACY SAFEGUARDS:
- Implement written information security program
- Encrypt all personal financial information
- Secure transmission of customer data
- Access controls based on business need
- Regular security assessments and audits

CUSTOMER INFORMATION PROTECTION:
- Social Security Numbers (SSN) - Highest protection level
- Bank account numbers and routing information
- Credit card and debit card numbers
- Income and employment information
- Credit scores and credit report data
- Asset and liability information
- Loan application details

DISCLOSURE REQUIREMENTS:
- Privacy notice at account opening
- Annual privacy notice to customers
- Opt-out notices for information sharing
- Clear explanation of information collection practices
- Third-party sharing limitations

DATA HANDLING PROTOCOLS:
- Limit access to authorized personnel only
- Log all access to customer information
- Secure disposal of customer information
- Vendor management and due diligence
- Incident response procedures

RETENTION REQUIREMENTS:
- Loan files: 25 years after loan closure
- Credit reports: 25 months after adverse action
- Privacy notices: 3 years
- Security logs: 6 years
- Training records: 3 years

EMPLOYEE TRAINING:
- Annual privacy and security training
- Role-specific access training
- Incident reporting procedures
- Customer interaction protocols
- Data handling best practices

PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE:
- Civil penalties up to $100,000 per violation
- Criminal penalties for willful violations
- Regulatory enforcement actions
- Reputational damage and customer loss
- Potential class-action lawsuits
