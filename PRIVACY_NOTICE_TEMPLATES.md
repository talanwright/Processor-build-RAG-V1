## 1. Initial Privacy Notice (Loan Application)

**When to send:** At the time of loan application submission
**Delivery method:** Email (automated via Make.com Scenario A)
**Retention:** Keep copy for 3 years

---

### Template: Initial Privacy Notice

```
Subject: Privacy Notice - Your Loan Application

Dear [Borrower Name],

Thank you for choosing [Your Company Name] for your loan application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and the security of your personal financial information.

This notice explains how we collect, use, and protect your information in compliance with federal law, specifically the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

We collect the following types of information:

• INFORMATION YOU PROVIDE:
  - Your name, address, email, and phone number
  - Social Security Number
  - Income and employment information
  - Asset and liability information
  - Bank account and financial information
  - Documents you upload (pay stubs, tax returns, bank statements, etc.)

• INFORMATION FROM OTHER SOURCES:
  - Credit reports from credit bureaus
  - Employment verification from employers
  - Bank verification from financial institutions

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOW DO WE USE YOUR INFORMATION?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

We use your information to:

• Process and evaluate your loan application
• Verify your income, employment, and financial information
• Determine your creditworthiness
• Comply with federal and state lending regulations
• Detect and prevent fraud
• Service your loan (if approved)
• Communicate with you about your application

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOW DO WE PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

✓ ENCRYPTION: All personal information is encrypted using military-grade
  AES-128 encryption both when stored and when transmitted over the internet

✓ SECURE SERVERS: Your data is stored on secure servers with restricted
  access, not publicly accessible from the internet

✓ ACCESS CONTROLS: Only authorized personnel with valid credentials can
  access your information

✓ AUDIT LOGGING: Every access to your information is logged with timestamps
  and user identification

✓ AUTOMATIC DELETION: Documents are securely deleted according to our
  retention policy

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHO DO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION WITH?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

We DO NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information with:

• SERVICE PROVIDERS who help us process your application:
  - Document processing systems
  - Credit reporting agencies
  - Employment verification services
  - Cloud hosting providers (Railway, AWS)

  All service providers are contractually required to protect your information
  and may only use it for the specific services they provide to us.

• LEGAL REQUIREMENTS:
  - We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or
    government investigation

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

You have the right to:

• REQUEST ACCESS to the information we have about you
• REQUEST CORRECTION of inaccurate information
• REQUEST DELETION of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
• OPT OUT of certain information sharing (see below)
• FILE A COMPLAINT with regulatory authorities if you believe your privacy
  rights have been violated

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OPT-OUT RIGHTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

You have the right to limit some sharing of your information.

To exercise your opt-out rights, contact us at:

📧 Email: [privacy@yourcompany.com]
📞 Phone: [Your Phone Number]
✉️  Mail: [Your Mailing Address]

Please include your name, loan application number, and specifically what
sharing you wish to opt out of.

NOTE: Even if you opt out, we may still share information:
- With service providers necessary to process your loan
- As required by law
- To prevent fraud

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DATA RETENTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

We retain your information as follows:

• Loan application files: 25 years after loan closure (as required by law)
• Credit reports: 25 months after adverse action
• Communication records: 6 years
• Audit logs: 6 years

You may request earlier deletion, but we must comply with legal retention
requirements for financial records.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CHANGES TO THIS NOTICE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

We will notify you if we make material changes to this privacy notice.
You will receive an updated notice:

• Annually (every 12 months)
• Within 30 days of any significant policy change
• If you request a copy

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
QUESTIONS OR CONCERNS?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

If you have questions about this privacy notice or our privacy practices:

📧 Email: [privacy@yourcompany.com]
📞 Phone: [Your Phone Number]
✉️  Mail: [Your Mailing Address]
🌐 Website: [Your Website]

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ACKNOWLEDGMENT

By continuing with your loan application, you acknowledge that you have
received and reviewed this Privacy Notice.

This notice was provided to you on: [Date]

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[Your Company Name]
[Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Phone] | [Email] | [Website]

This privacy notice complies with the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) and
applicable state privacy laws.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

## 5. Website Privacy Policy (Full)

**Where to post:** On your company website at `/privacy`
**Update frequency:** Review annually

---

### Template: Full Privacy Policy

```markdown
# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** [Date]
**Last Updated:** [Date]

## Introduction

[Your Company Name] ("we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your
information when you apply for a loan through our services.

This policy complies with:
- Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA)
- Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)
- Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA)
- State privacy laws

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- Full name
- Social Security Number
- Date of birth
- Contact information (address, phone, email)
- Government-issued ID

### Financial Information
- Income and employment details
- Bank account information
- Assets and liabilities
- Credit history and credit score
- Tax information

### Documents
- Pay stubs
- Bank statements
- Tax returns (W-2s, 1040s)
- Employment verification letters
- Proof of assets

### Automatically Collected Information
- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Access times and dates
- Pages viewed

## 2. How We Collect Information

We collect information:
- **Directly from you** when you submit your loan application
- **From credit bureaus** when we pull your credit report
- **From employers** when we verify employment
- **From banks** when we verify assets
- **Automatically** through our website and systems

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Evaluate your loan application
- Verify your identity, income, and creditworthiness
- Process and service your loan
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
- Prevent fraud and unauthorized transactions
- Improve our services
- Communicate with you about your application

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR, if applicable)

We process your information based on:
- **Your consent** - You provide information voluntarily for loan processing
- **Contractual necessity** - To fulfill our loan agreement with you
- **Legal obligation** - To comply with financial regulations
- **Legitimate interests** - To prevent fraud and improve services

## 5. How We Share Your Information

### We DO NOT sell your personal information

We may share your information with:

**Service Providers:**
- Cloud hosting providers (Railway, AWS)
- Document processing systems
- Email service providers (Gmail, Make.com)
- Analytics providers

**Financial Institutions:**
- Credit bureaus (for credit reports)
- Banks (for employment/income verification)
- Secondary market investors (if loan is sold)

**Legal Requirements:**
- Government agencies (as required by law)
- Law enforcement (with valid subpoena)
- Regulators (for compliance audits)

**With Your Consent:**
- Other third parties with your explicit permission

All service providers are contractually bound to protect your information.

## 6. How We Protect Your Information

### Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption at rest:** AES-128 encryption for all stored data
- **Encryption in transit:** TLS 1.2+ for all data transmission
- **Access controls:** Multi-factor authentication required
- **Audit logging:** All access logged and monitored
- **Secure infrastructure:** Private database, firewalls, intrusion detection

### Administrative Safeguards
- Background checks for employees with data access
- Security awareness training
- Incident response procedures
- Regular security audits
- Data minimization practices

### Physical Safeguards
- Cloud infrastructure with SOC 2 Type II certification
- Restricted physical access to servers
- Encrypted backups

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your information as follows:

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Loan application files | 25 years after loan closure | Federal requirement |
| Credit reports | 25 months after adverse action | FCRA requirement |
| Communication records | 6 years | GLBA requirement |
| Security logs | 6 years | GLBA requirement |
| Training records | 3 years | Compliance |

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

### Access
- Request a copy of the information we have about you
- Receive information in a portable format

### Correction
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Update your contact information

### Deletion
- Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)

### Opt-Out
- Limit sharing for marketing purposes
- Unsubscribe from promotional emails

### Object
- Object to certain processing of your information

### Portability
- Request transfer of your information to another service

### Withdraw Consent
- Withdraw consent for processing (where applicable)

**To exercise your rights:**
- Email: privacy@yourcompany.com
- Phone: [Your Phone Number]
- Mail: [Your Address]

We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.

## 9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights:

- **Right to Know:** What personal information we collect and how we use it
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal information
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Opt out of sale of personal information (we don't sell)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** Equal service regardless of privacy choices

**California Opt-Out:** Call 1-[Phone] or email privacy@yourcompany.com

## 10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly
collect information from children. If we learn we have collected information
from a child under 18, we will delete it immediately.

## 11. International Data Transfers

Your information is stored on servers in the United States. If you are accessing
our services from outside the U.S., please be aware that your information may be
transferred to, stored, and processed in the U.S.

## 12. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use:
- **Essential cookies:** Required for system functionality
- **Analytics cookies:** To understand how our website is used
- **Security cookies:** To detect fraudulent activity

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## 13. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for
the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their privacy policies.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We will notify you of material changes:
- By email to the address on file
- By posting a notice on our website
- Within 30 days before the change takes effect

You will receive an updated privacy notice annually.

## 15. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify you:
- Within 60 days of discovering the breach (or as required by state law)
- Via email and/or postal mail
- Including description of the breach, information affected, and steps we're taking

## 16. Contact Information

### Privacy Officer
Email: privacy@yourcompany.com
Phone: [Your Phone Number]
Mail: [Your Company Name]
      [Address Line 1]
      [Address Line 2]
      [City, State ZIP]

### Complaints

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated:

1. Contact our Privacy Officer (details above)
2. File a complaint with the FTC: ftc.gov/complaint
3. File a complaint with CFPB: consumerfinance.gov/complaint
4. Contact your state Attorney General

## 17. Regulatory Information

We are subject to oversight by:
- Federal Trade Commission (FTC)
- Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)
- [State] Department of Financial Institutions

## 18. Acknowledgment

By using our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this
Privacy Policy.

---

**Effective Date:** [Date]
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Reviewed:** [Date]

[Your Company Name]
[Address]
[Phone] | [Email] | [Website]

---

*This privacy policy was created in compliance with the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act
(GLBA), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other applicable privacy laws.*
```
