INCOME VERIFICATION RED FLAGS

EMPLOYMENT RED FLAGS:
- Employment start date less than 2 years ago (requires explanation)
- Frequent job changes within same industry
- Employment gaps longer than 30 days without explanation
- Employer unable to verify employment
- Round numbers on pay stubs (indicates potential fraud)
- Inconsistent employer information across documents

PAY STUB RED FLAGS:
- Pay stub dates outside required 30-day window
- Handwritten or altered pay stubs
- Poor print quality or formatting inconsistencies
- Missing employer information or tax withholdings
- Year-to-date figures that don't align with pay periods
- Base pay inconsistent with hourly rate calculations

INCOME DISCREPANCIES:
- Application income differs from pay stub income by >10%
- W-2 income doesn't align with pay stub year-to-date
- Tax return income significantly lower than stated income
- Overtime/bonus income not sustainable (less than 2 years history)
- Commission income without 2-year average

SELF-EMPLOYED RED FLAGS:
- Tax returns showing losses but claiming positive cash flow
- Significant depreciation reducing net income
- Business expenses that seem excessive or personal
- Inconsistent business income year over year
- Unable to provide business bank statements

DOCUMENTATION RED FLAGS:
- Documents from unknown or suspicious sources
- Missing pages from tax returns or bank statements
- Different fonts or formatting within same document
- Photocopied documents when originals required
- Employer verification letters on personal letterhead

VERIFICATION ISSUES:
- Employer phone number goes to voicemail or personal phone
- HR department cannot verify employment details
- Employment verification contradicts pay stub information
- Employer reluctant to provide written verification
- Third-party payroll company cannot verify employment

INCOME CALCULATION RED FLAGS:
- Using gross income when net income more appropriate
- Including non-recurring income sources
- Counting temporary or contract income as permanent
- Double-counting income sources
- Failing to deduct required business expenses
