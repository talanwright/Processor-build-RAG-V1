CONVENTIONAL LOAN REQUIREMENTS

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Loan Application (1003 Form)
2. Pay Stubs (Most recent 30 days)
3. W-2 Forms (Last 2 years)
4. Tax Returns (Last 2 years with all schedules)
5. Bank Statements (Last 2 months, all accounts)
6. Employment Verification Letter (VOE)
7. Asset Verification (VOD for large deposits)
8. Credit Report (Tri-merge, min 620 score)
9. Appraisal Report
10. Title Report/Commitment
11. Homeowners Insurance Declaration
12. Property Tax Records

INCOME REQUIREMENTS:
- Stable employment history (2+ years same field)
- Debt-to-income ratio maximum 43-45%
- Gross monthly income documentation required
- Self-employed: 2 years tax returns + profit/loss statements

ASSET REQUIREMENTS:
- Down payment: Minimum 3-5%
- Closing costs: 2-5% of loan amount
- Reserves: 2 months PITI recommended
- Gift funds: Allowed with gift letter
- Source of funds documentation required

PROPERTY REQUIREMENTS:
- Owner-occupied primary residence
- Investment properties allowed (higher down payment)
- Appraisal must meet or exceed contract price
- Property condition standards apply

CREDIT REQUIREMENTS:
- Minimum 620 FICO score
- No bankruptcy within 4 years
- No foreclosure within 7 years
- Maximum 2 30-day late payments in 12 months
- Debt service coverage ratio compliance

RED FLAGS TO WATCH:
- Recent large deposits without explanation
- Employment gaps longer than 30 days
- Credit score drops during process
- Income discrepancies between documents
- Property value below contract price
- Undisclosed debt obligations
