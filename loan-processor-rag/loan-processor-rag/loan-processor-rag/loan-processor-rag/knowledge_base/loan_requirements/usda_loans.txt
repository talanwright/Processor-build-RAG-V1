USDA RURAL DEVELOPMENT LOAN REQUIREMENTS

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Loan Application (1003 Form)
2. Pay Stubs (Most recent 30 days)
3. W-2 Forms (Last 2 years)
4. Tax Returns (Last 2 years with all schedules)
5. Bank Statements (Last 2 months, all accounts)
6. Employment Verification Letter (VOE)
7. Asset Verification (VOD for large deposits)
8. Credit Report (Tri-merge, min 640 score recommended)
9. USDA Appraisal Report
10. Title Report/Commitment
11. Homeowners Insurance Declaration
12. Household Income Certification
13. Income Verification from all household members

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
- Property must be in USDA-eligible rural area
- Household income must not exceed area limits (typically 115% of median)
- Must be U.S. citizen, permanent resident, or qualified alien
- Property must be primary residence (owner-occupied)
- Cannot own other adequate housing
- Applicant must demonstrate repayment ability

INCOME REQUIREMENTS:
- Household income limits vary by county and household size
- Adjusted annual income used for eligibility
- All household members 18+ must have income verified
- Stable employment history (2+ years same field)
- Debt-to-income ratio maximum 41% (29% front-end)
- Self-employed: 2 years tax returns + profit/loss statements
- Overtime/Bonus income: 2 year history required

ASSET REQUIREMENTS:
- Down payment: 0% required (100% financing available)
- Closing costs: Can be paid by seller (up to 6% concessions)
- Reserves: Not required but recommended
- Gift funds: Allowed with proper documentation
- Liquid assets cannot exceed certain limits for 100% financing
- All funds must be sourced and documented

PROPERTY REQUIREMENTS:
- Property must be in USDA-eligible rural area (check eligibility map)
- Must be primary residence (owner-occupied)
- Property must be modest in size, design, and cost
- Property must meet USDA property standards
- Maximum acreage restrictions apply
- Cannot have in-ground swimming pool
- 1-4 unit properties eligible (with income restrictions)
- New construction allowed

CREDIT REQUIREMENTS:
- No minimum credit score for manual underwriting
- Automated underwriting: 640+ FICO score recommended
- No bankruptcy within 3 years (Chapter 7)
- No bankruptcy within 1 year (Chapter 13 with payment history)
- No foreclosure within 3 years
- Clean 12-month housing payment history required
- Collections: Must be paid or have payment arrangement
- Manual underwriting available for credit scores below 640

USDA-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS:
- Upfront Guarantee Fee: 1% of loan amount (can be financed)
- Annual Fee: 0.35% of outstanding balance
- Property location verification required (USDA eligibility map)
- Income limits apply to entire household
- Adjusted income calculation includes deductions
- Property must meet HUD property standards
- Cannot exceed maximum loan limits for area

INCOME LIMITS (Examples - vary by county):
- Check USDA website for specific county limits
- Generally 115% of area median income
- Adjusted for household size
- Includes all household members 18 and older
- Certain income sources excluded from calculation

PROPERTY LOCATION ELIGIBILITY:
- Rural areas defined by USDA
- Populations under 35,000 eligible
- Some suburban areas may qualify
- Use USDA eligibility map for verification
- Properties in cities over 10,000 population generally not eligible
- Exceptions for census tracts meeting income requirements

RED FLAGS TO WATCH:
- Property not in eligible rural area
- Household income exceeds limits
- Ownership of other adequate housing
- Property has in-ground swimming pool
- Property exceeds modest size/cost guidelines
- Credit score below 640 (requires manual underwriting)
- Housing payment history issues
- Income documentation inconsistencies
- Property condition issues
- Excessive acreage for residential use

COMPENSATING FACTORS:
- Excellent credit history
- Minimal increase in housing payment
- Significant cash reserves
- Low debt-to-income ratio
- Stable employment (5+ years)
- Conservative credit use
- Previous successful homeownership
- Property in high-demand area

MANUAL UNDERWRITING REQUIREMENTS:
- Required for credit scores below 640
- Must meet all compensating factor requirements
- No more than 1 30-day late payment in past 12 months
- No 60-day late payments in past 24 months
- All derogatory credit must be explained
- DTI typically limited to 41%

SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS:
- No monthly mortgage insurance (major benefit vs FHA)
- Lower annual fee than FHA MIP
- 100% financing available
- Seller concessions up to 6%
- Repair/improvement financing available
- Refinancing options available for existing USDA loans
- Geographic restrictions limit property choices
- Income restrictions limit eligibility
