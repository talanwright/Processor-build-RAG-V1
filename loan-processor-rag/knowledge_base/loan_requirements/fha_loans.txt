FHA LOAN REQUIREMENTS

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Loan Application (1003 Form)
2. Pay Stubs (Most recent 30 days)
3. W-2 Forms (Last 2 years)
4. Tax Returns (Last 2 years with all schedules)
5. Bank Statements (Last 2 months, all accounts)
6. Employment Verification Letter (VOE)
7. Asset Verification (VOD for large deposits)
8. Credit Report (Tri-merge, min 580 score)
9. FHA Appraisal Report (must meet FHA property standards)
10. Title Report/Commitment
11. Homeowners Insurance Declaration
12. FHA Self-Sufficiency Test (for 2-4 unit properties)

INCOME REQUIREMENTS:
- Stable employment history (2+ years same field preferred)
- Debt-to-income ratio maximum 43% (may go to 50% with compensating factors)
- Front-end ratio maximum 31%
- Gross monthly income documentation required
- Self-employed: 2 years tax returns + profit/loss statements + business license
- Overtime/Bonus income: 2 year history required

ASSET REQUIREMENTS:
- Down payment: Minimum 3.5% (credit score 580+)
- Down payment: Minimum 10% (credit score 500-579)
- Closing costs: Can be paid by seller (up to 6% of sales price)
- Reserves: Not required but recommended
- Gift funds: Allowed from family members with gift letter
- All funds must be sourced and seasoned (60 days)
- Down payment assistance programs allowed

PROPERTY REQUIREMENTS:
- Must be primary residence (owner-occupied)
- Property must meet FHA minimum property standards
- FHA appraisal required (safety, security, soundness)
- Property condition requirements more strict than conventional
- No flipped properties (must be owned 90 days)
- Maximum loan limits by county
- 1-4 unit properties eligible

CREDIT REQUIREMENTS:
- Minimum 580 FICO score (3.5% down)
- Minimum 500 FICO score (10% down)
- No bankruptcy within 2 years (Chapter 7)
- No bankruptcy within 1 year (Chapter 13 with payment history)
- No foreclosure within 3 years
- Manual underwriting required for scores below 620
- Housing payment history critical (no 30-day lates in 12 months)

FHA-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS:
- Upfront Mortgage Insurance Premium (UFMIP): 1.75% of base loan
- Annual Mortgage Insurance Premium (MIP) required
- MIP duration: Life of loan if down payment < 10%
- MIP duration: 11 years if down payment >= 10%
- Property must meet HUD guidelines
- Lead-based paint inspection for homes built before 1978

RED FLAGS TO WATCH:
- Credit score below 580 (requires manual underwriting)
- Recent collections or charge-offs not paid
- Employment gaps without explanation
- Large undocumented deposits
- Property condition issues on appraisal
- DTI above 43% without compensating factors
- Recent inquiries indicating new debt
- Insufficient reserves for investment properties

COMPENSATING FACTORS:
- Minimal increase in housing payment
- Significant cash reserves
- Conservative credit use
- Residual income exceeds guideline by 20%
- Borrower receiving homeownership counseling
- Previous successful homeownership
