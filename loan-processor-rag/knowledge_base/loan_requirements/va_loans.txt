VA LOAN REQUIREMENTS

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Loan Application (1003 Form)
2. Certificate of Eligibility (COE) - REQUIRED for VA loans
3. Pay Stubs (Most recent 30 days)
4. W-2 Forms (Last 2 years)
5. Tax Returns (Last 2 years with all schedules)
6. Bank Statements (Last 2 months, all accounts)
7. Employment Verification Letter (VOE)
8. DD-214 Form (discharge papers for veterans)
9. Statement of Service (for active duty)
10. VA Appraisal Report (Notice of Value)
11. Title Report/Commitment
12. Homeowners Insurance Declaration

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
- Active duty service member (90+ days)
- Veteran with honorable discharge
- National Guard/Reserve (6+ years service)
- Surviving spouse of veteran who died in service or from service-connected disability
- Must obtain Certificate of Eligibility (COE) from VA
- Sufficient entitlement available for loan amount

INCOME REQUIREMENTS:
- Stable and reliable income required
- No maximum DTI ratio, but typically 41% guideline
- Residual income requirement (varies by region and family size)
- Employment history (2+ years same field)
- Self-employed: 2 years tax returns + profit/loss statements
- Military income: BAH, BAS, flight pay, hazard pay can be used

ASSET REQUIREMENTS:
- Down payment: 0% required (100% financing available)
- Closing costs: Can be paid by seller (up to 4% concessions)
- VA Funding Fee: 2.15-3.3% (can be financed)
- VA Funding Fee waived for disabled veterans
- Reserves: Not required but may be needed for high DTI
- Gift funds: Allowed with proper documentation

PROPERTY REQUIREMENTS:
- Must be primary residence (owner-occupied)
- Property must meet VA Minimum Property Requirements (MPRs)
- VA appraisal required (Notice of Value issued)
- Property must be move-in ready
- No safety hazards or structural issues
- Well/septic systems must pass inspection
- 1-4 unit properties eligible
- Condos must be VA-approved

CREDIT REQUIREMENTS:
- No minimum credit score required by VA
- Most lenders require 620+ FICO score
- No bankruptcy within 2 years
- No foreclosure within 2 years
- Clean payment history preferred
- Collections/charge-offs: Must be resolved or have payment plan
- Compensating factors considered

VA-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS:
- VA Funding Fee (percentage varies by service type, down payment, first-time use)
- Funding Fee: 2.15% (first-time use, 0% down)
- Funding Fee: 3.3% (subsequent use, 0% down)
- Funding Fee: 1.25-2.15% (with 5%+ down payment)
- Exempt from funding fee: Veterans with service-connected disability
- Certificate of Reasonable Value (CRV) required
- Termite inspection required in certain states
- No prepayment penalties allowed

ENTITLEMENT CALCULATIONS:
- Basic entitlement: $36,000
- Additional entitlement: Up to $144,000+ (varies by county)
- Full entitlement: Allows no down payment
- Partial entitlement: May require down payment
- Restoration of entitlement: Available after selling property or refinancing

RED FLAGS TO WATCH:
- Missing or invalid Certificate of Eligibility
- Insufficient entitlement for loan amount
- Property fails VA MPRs
- Residual income below guidelines
- Recent bankruptcy or foreclosure
- Type of discharge (must be honorable)
- Property condition issues
- Undisclosed debt obligations

SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS:
- No mortgage insurance required (major benefit)
- Can reuse VA benefit after paying off loan
- Can have multiple VA loans simultaneously
- Adapted housing grants available for disabled veterans
- Energy efficiency improvements can be financed
- Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan (IRRRL) available
