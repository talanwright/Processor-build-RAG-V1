TRID (TILA-RESPA INTEGRATED DISCLOSURE) GUIDELINES

OVERVIEW:
TRID combines Truth in Lending Act (TILA) and Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA) requirements into integrated disclosures for residential mortgage transactions.

APPLICABILITY:
- Closed-end consumer credit transactions secured by real property
- Does NOT apply to: HELOC, reverse mortgages, mobile homes not attached to land, loans by person/entity making 5 or fewer mortgages per year
- Applies to purchases, refinances, construction loans, assumption transactions

REQUIRED DISCLOSURES:

1. LOAN ESTIMATE (LE)
- Must be provided within 3 business days of application
- Valid for 10 business days (with certain exceptions)
- Cannot be issued before borrower provides 6 pieces of information: name, income, SSN, property address, estimate of property value, desired loan amount

2. CLOSING DISCLOSURE (CD)
- Must be provided at least 3 business days before consummation
- Provides final loan terms and closing costs
- Must be in borrower's possession 3 business days before closing

TIMING REQUIREMENTS:

Application to Loan Estimate:
- LE must be delivered within 3 business days of receiving application
- Business days = all calendar days except Sundays and legal public holidays
- Delivery methods: hand delivery, mail (add 3 days), email (if consented)

Loan Estimate to Closing Disclosure:
- CD must be received at least 3 business days before consummation
- Business days = all calendar days except Sundays and legal public holidays
- 3-day waiting period cannot be waived except in bona fide personal financial emergency

Closing Disclosure Changes Requiring New 3-Day Wait:
1. APR increases by more than 0.125% for most loans (0.25% for irregular loans)
2. Loan product changes (e.g., fixed to ARM)
3. Prepayment penalty is added

REVISED DISCLOSURES:

When Revised LE is Required:
- Changed circumstances affecting eligibility or value
- Changed circumstances affecting estimated charges (outside 10% tolerance)
- Borrower-requested changes
- Interest rate lock/expiration
- Construction loan conversion
- New information affecting disclosures

Tolerance Categories:

Zero Tolerance (Cannot Increase):
- Lender's charges for origination
- Charges for services borrower cannot shop for
- Transfer taxes

10% Cumulative Tolerance:
- Recording fees
- Charges for third-party services borrower can shop for (if borrower does not select provider from lender's list)

No Tolerance Limit (Can Change):
- Prepaid interest
- Property insurance premiums
- Amounts placed in escrow
- Charges for third-party services borrower can shop for (if borrower selects provider from lender's list)
- Charges paid to affiliates when borrower was permitted to shop

CHANGED CIRCUMSTANCES:

Valid Changed Circumstances:
1. Acts of God, war, disaster, or other emergency
2. Information specific to borrower or transaction that was relied upon, changes or is found inaccurate after LE issued
3. New information specific to borrower or transaction not relied upon when LE issued
4. Borrower-requested changes
5. Interest rate lock or expiration
6. Construction loan conversion
7. Delayed settlement date (beyond 60 days)

Documentation Required:
- Written description of changed circumstance
- Supporting documentation
- Revised LE with explanation
- Maintain in loan file for 5 years

RECORD RETENTION:
- All TRID-related documents must be retained for 5 years after consummation
- Evidence of compliance required
- Documentation of changed circumstances
- Proof of delivery/receipt of disclosures

COMMON VIOLATIONS:

1. TIMING VIOLATIONS:
- LE not delivered within 3 business days of application
- CD not provided 3 business days before consummation
- Closing before 3-day waiting period expires

2. CONTENT VIOLATIONS:
- Incorrect fee amounts outside tolerance limits
- Missing required information
- Incorrect APR calculation
- Failure to disclose fees

3. PROCEDURAL VIOLATIONS:
- Invalid reason for revised LE
- Insufficient documentation of changed circumstances
- Failure to reset 3-day waiting period when required
- Improper tolerance category assignment

4. DISCLOSURE VIOLATIONS:
- CD not in borrower's possession 3 days before closing
- Incorrect delivery method documentation
- Failure to provide revised CD when required

RED FLAGS TO WATCH:

1. TIMING RED FLAGS:
- Rushing application to closing in less than 7 business days
- Insufficient time between LE and CD
- Last-minute changes to loan terms
- Pressure to close before 3-day period expires

2. FEE RED FLAGS:
- Fees increasing beyond tolerance limits without valid changed circumstance
- Missing documentation for fee changes
- Inconsistent fees between LE and CD
- Fees not properly categorized

3. DOCUMENTATION RED FLAGS:
- Missing proof of LE/CD delivery
- No documentation for changed circumstances
- Incomplete application file
- Missing borrower acknowledgments

4. COMPLIANCE RED FLAGS:
- Pattern of tolerance violations
- Frequent revised LEs without valid reasons
- APR miscalculations
- Incorrect service provider categorization

BEST PRACTICES:

1. APPLICATION STAGE:
- Collect all 6 application elements before issuing LE
- Document receipt date and time
- Provide LE within required timeframe
- Use accurate estimates from reliable sources

2. PROCESSING STAGE:
- Monitor for changed circumstances
- Document all changes thoroughly
- Issue revised LEs when required
- Track tolerance categories carefully

3. CLOSING STAGE:
- Prepare CD in advance
- Allow sufficient time for 3-day waiting period
- Coordinate with all parties on timeline
- Document CD delivery method and date

4. ONGOING COMPLIANCE:
- Regular staff training on TRID requirements
- Audit files for compliance
- Maintain documentation for 5 years
- Update procedures as regulations change

PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS:
- Individual violations: Up to $5,000 per day
- Pattern or practice violations: Up to $25,000 per day
- Knowing violations: Criminal penalties possible
- Civil liability for damages
- Borrower right to rescission in certain cases

CFPB EXAMINATION FOCUS:
- Accurate and timely delivery of LE and CD
- Proper fee tolerance compliance
- Valid changed circumstances documentation
- 3-day waiting period compliance
- Record retention compliance
- Staff training and procedures
